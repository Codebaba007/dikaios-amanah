\subsection{Overview}

The budget estimation represents the approximate cost required to establish and operate Dikaios Amanah as a functional digital investment platform beyond the prototype stage. Existing development hardware and open-source software are assumed to be available and are therefore excluded from the estimation. The budget focuses on infrastructure, deployment, legal compliance, business registration, security, and operational expenses. Market prices are based on publicly available information from relevant vendors and service providers.

\subsection{Budget Justification}

The proposed budget reflects the estimated expenditure required to transition the platform from an academic prototype to an operational software service. The largest expenses relate to legal compliance, business registration, cybersecurity, identity verification, and cloud infrastructure, which are essential for operating an online investment platform.

The MVP developed as part of this capstone project will not incur all of these expenses because it uses open-source technologies and simulated services. However, these estimated costs demonstrate the financial considerations involved in commercializing the proposed platform and are supported by publicly available pricing from relevant vendors and service providers.

The use of open-source technologies including React.js, Node.js, Express.js, PostgreSQL, Git, and Visual Studio Code eliminates software licensing fees while maintaining a modern and scalable technology stack.
